I'm Isaac Lee, and I run Lee Tennis Co. in Irvine. I'm adding other coaches to the site. You choose where you teach and what you charge, students book you from your own listing, and you keep 90% of every lesson that comes from the site.

How it works

Your court
Teach at Toscana, my home court in Irvine, or choose your own. Your own court has to be one where you're allowed to give paid lessons.
Your rate
You set what you charge.
The 10%
You collect from your students. At the end of each month, you pay me 10% of what you earned from lessons booked through the site, by Zelle or in cash. Students you already have don't count.
Your listing
Your photo, bio, court and rates go on leetennisco.com, with your own booking link.

Irvine's city courts are a special case. The City only lets its listed instructors teach on them, and it treats unauthorized paid lessons as a misdemeanor. If you aren't on that list, choose Toscana or a private, club or school court.
