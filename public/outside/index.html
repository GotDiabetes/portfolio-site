Outside work

Most of it happens early or late, and most of it happens with other people. That is usually the point.

None of this is on my résumé, but it is where most of my week goes once the laptop shuts. It is also where I have learned the most about getting people to show up for something regularly, which turns out to be the same problem in a running club and in a sales pipeline.

RunningCamino Fidei

The running club I started at church

Founder

  • Camino Fidei is the first running club my church has had. I started it because a few of us were already running separately, and it seemed obvious that we should be doing it together.
  • We run at night. Nobody gets dropped, and the pace is set by whoever turns up slowest — which is the only rule that has mattered.
