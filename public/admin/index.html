On the site

  • Your numbers

    What people did on the site, by day and language, and the chat questions it couldn't answer. Asks for your stats password.

  • Drill library

    About 200 drills and games by age and skill, the faults they fix, an age-by-age guide, your students and a lesson-plan builder, with 300+ checked videos.

  • Lesson recap

    After a lesson, tick what you worked on and it writes a short note for the player or parent, in their language.

  • Gift card

    Make a printable card when someone buys lessons as a gift.

  • Flyer

    The notice-board flyer with a QR code and tear-off strips, in any of the site's languages.
