About

Fundamentals first, then everything else

I've been coaching for three years. I started as a Junior Pro at LifeSport Libertyville, then joined the Pro Staff of Grip N Rip, the junior program at Newport Beach Tennis Club.

Most of my work is with developing juniors — getting the fundamentals right early so progress keeps compounding. I also run adult clinics for players who want more consistency and a clearer plan once points actually start.

I played four years of high school varsity. Coaching is a part-time job for me, fitted around school, so the schedule stays small and deliberate.
