About

Fundamentals first, then everything else

I've been coaching for three years. I started as a Junior Pro at LifeSport Libertyville, then joined the Pro Staff of Grip N Rip, the junior program at Newport Beach Tennis Club.

Most of my work is with developing juniors — getting the fundamentals right early so progress keeps compounding. I also run adult clinics for players who want more consistency and a clearer plan once points actually start.

I played four years of high school varsity and now play college club tennis.

Lessons

Four ways to work together

Every player starts somewhere different. Sessions are shaped around your level and how often you can get on court.

Private lessons

$80/hour

One-on-one for juniors and adults alike. Stroke and serve mechanics, footwork, ball-progression fundamentals for younger players, and a practice plan you can actually work on between sessions.

Semi-private lessons

$110/hour for two — $55 each

The same lesson, split between two players of a similar level. You each pay less than a private, and the pairing gives you live rallies and points to work through rather than feeding alone. Bring a friend, a sibling, or a doubles partner.

Hitting partner sessions

$45/hour

Straight hitting rather than instruction, for players who just want a reliable ball to work against. Sessions run 60 minutes or longer.
