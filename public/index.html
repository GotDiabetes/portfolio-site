About

Fundamentals first, then everything else

I've been coaching for three years. I started as a Junior Pro at LifeSport Libertyville, then joined the Pro Staff of Grip N Rip, the junior program at Newport Beach Tennis Club.

I'm certified by the RSPA, the Racket Sports Professionals Association (formerly the USPTA), and approved under USTA Safe Play: background-checked and SafeSport-trained.

Most of my work is with developing juniors — getting the fundamentals right early so progress keeps compounding. I also run adult clinics for players who want more consistency and a clearer plan once points actually start.

I played four years of high school varsity. Coaching is a part-time job for me, fitted around school, so the schedule stays small and deliberate.

The court

The court you'll learn on

Hover or tap a part of the court to see what we work on there.

Baseline

Where most of the lesson lives. Groundstrokes, footwork and rally tolerance: ten balls in a row before you try to hit one hard.

Service boxes

Serves and returns. The toss and the rhythm come first, then the spin, and you serve at a target rather than just over the net.

The net

Volleys and overheads. Short swings, early contact, and the footwork to get there in time.

Mid-court

The transition zone: approach shots and the half-volley, the shots that take you from the baseline to the net.

The alleys

Doubles. Angles, poaching and where to stand, for anyone playing league or social doubles.

Questions people ask

Does my child need to have played before?

No. Juniors start from complete beginners. The youngest learn on slower, softer balls (red, then orange, then green) and move up as they're ready.

Is my child safe with you?

I'm approved under USTA Safe Play, which means I'm background-checked and SafeSport-trained. I'm also certified by the RSPA, the Racket Sports Professionals Association.

Do I pay when I book?

No. Booking online just holds the time: no card, no deposit. You pay in person.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or move a lesson with 24 hours' notice; later cancellations are charged. If rain or heat cancels a lesson, we reschedule at no cost.

Can you come to my court or my home?

No. All lessons are at my home court, the Toscana Apartments tennis courts in Irvine.

I don't have a racket. Is that okay?

Yes. You can borrow one of mine.

Are there lesson packages?

Yes, for private lessons: 5 for $375 or 10 for $700, paid in person. That's $75 and $70 an hour instead of $80.

Can I give lessons as a gift?

Yes: one private lesson, 5 or 10 private lessons, or a hitting session. Send me the request, we settle payment in person, and you get a printable gift card.

Why a younger coach?

Fair question. I've coached since 2023, first as a Junior Pro at LifeSport Libertyville and then on the Pro Staff of Grip N Rip at Newport Beach Tennis Club. I'm RSPA-certified and Safe Play approved, and I played four years of high school varsity. Book one lesson and judge for yourself.
