# Setting up your Google Business Profile

When someone searches "tennis lessons Irvine", Google shows the map listings
first. A free Business Profile is what puts you there, and it is where your
reviews live. Allow about 15 minutes, plus Google's verification step.

## Before you start: one decision

Google asks whether customers visit your address. Lessons are at the Toscana
Apartments courts, a private complex. **Decide whether you want that address
on Google Maps.** If you'd rather not (or until Toscana's management has
said yes to paid lessons in writing), choose the option that hides your
address and lists **Irvine, CA** as your service area. The website already
names Toscana, so nothing is lost.

## Steps

1. Go to **google.com/business** and sign in with the account you use for
   lessons (isaacleetennis@gmail.com).
2. **Business name:** `Lee Tennis Co.` Use only the real name, with no
   extra words like "Best Irvine Tennis Lessons". Google suspends profiles
   for that.
3. **Category:** `Tennis instructor`. Type it in and pick it from the
   list. If it isn't offered, choose the closest coaching category.
4. **Location:** see the decision above.
5. **Website:** `https://leetennisco.com`. A phone number is optional.
6. **Verify.** Google will ask you to prove the business is real, often
   with a short video or a code. Follow its steps; it can take a few days.
7. **Fill in the rest** using the text below: description, services and
   prices, and your usual hours (only you know those).
8. **Photos:** upload a few from `public/images/` in this folder:
   `pointing.jpg`, `coaching-clinic.jpg`, `serve.jpg` and `forehand.jpg`.

## Text to paste

**Description** (Google allows 750 characters; this is about 640):

> Private tennis lessons in Irvine with Isaac Lee, an RSPA-certified coach

## After it's verified: your review link

1. In your profile, find **Ask for reviews** (sometimes "Get more
   reviews"). It gives you a short link, like `https://g.page/r/…/review`.
2. Send me that link. It goes in two places, and both then switch on by
   themselves:
   - `public/index.html`: the `review-link` tag. "Leave a review" appears
     under the reviews on every language's page.
   - `public/recap/index.html`: the same tag. The lesson recap gets an "Ask
     for a Google review" box.
3. Ask the families you've coached. Reviews have to come from real
   students or parents, and **you can't offer anything in return for one**
   (a discount or a free lesson). Google removes reviews that break that
   rule.
